Mini Music Maker

An interactive music project created with p5.js. Play different notes using your keyboard or mouse and watch the colorful animations react.

How to Play

Press A, S, D, F, G, H, or J on your keyboard, or click the piano keys to play different notes. Try combining notes to create your own melody!

Homework 12 - Sound Exploration

Sounds created using the p5.sound synthesizer. No external audio files were used.
